4️⃣ South African Device Check

iStore only accepts eligible South African devices for trade-in. Before proceeding, verify that the device is a South African model.

How to check
  1. Open Settings → General → About.
  2. Find Model Number.
  3. Tap the Model Number once to reveal the Apple Part Number (example: MYE93QN/A).
  4. Confirm that the part number matches an accepted South African regional code according to the current iStore trade-in requirements.
